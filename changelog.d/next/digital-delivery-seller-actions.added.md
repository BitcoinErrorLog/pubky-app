Sellers deliver emailed and message-kind digital items from the order: a paid order waiting on them reads "To deliver", Show email reveals the buyer's address with how to use it, and Mark emailed or Mark delivered completes delivery. Every paid digital order shows its delivery evidence, such as "Delivered automatically · first opened Sep 26, 14:02 · opened 3 times" or "Marked emailed Sep 26, 14:05". Sellers can record a refund on a delivered or completed digital order, and approving a cancel says opened files stay counted as sold.
